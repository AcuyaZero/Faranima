Faranima Chapter 2

*Panting* *Panting*

I hear my heart pounding in my chest, and I can feel the sweat dripping down my face. I stared to Gina who looks more exhausted than I am.

Emily: "Gina, are you okay? We should take a break."

Gina: "No... I'm okay just... legs... tired... haaah....."

Gina protests but continues to walk, her breathing heavy and labored, concern etched on my face.

Gina: "Emily... I'm... tireeeed..."

Gina turned to me. A poor girl begging to rest.

Emily: "Hang on Gina, we're almost there! Just a little further..."

Gina: "I... I wait... just... ahhhhh... Let me just catch my breath..."

Gina placed her hands on her knees, trying to steady her breathing. I scanned the interior of the school, looking for our room.

My eyes ended up on a blue classroom door. I approached it and read the sign next to it.

YEAR II - Jose Rizal

Emily: "Yes! This is it!"

I scanned the student lists that is placed under the section name. I found my surname:

Densen

I twisted the nose doorknob and the chill air embraces me as soon as the gap widens. A male teacher greeted me with a book on his side. His glasses proved that he is an experienced teacher.

Teacher: "Well hello there, I think you're the last student on the lists that has yet to arrive?"

Emily: "Uh-Uhm y-yes sir." I fumbled on my words for some reason.

Teacher: "Well, you can take a seat, we're already starting assembly. But next time, this would be considered as tardiness."

I nodded and was about to enter the classroom when I remembered Gina. I gave my excuse to the teacher and went back to the hallway. She's less exhausted now, but still visibly tired. I looked at the list again and searched for her surname.

Ramos... But there was no Ramos here...

Gina looked at me with an unreadable expression, then turned to look at me and said,

Gina: "Emily, guess I'm not in the same class as with you."

Forcing herself to chuckle on the situation she was in.

Gina: "I guess I have to go to another class, but I'll figure it out myself. Don't worry about me."

She reassures me. But I can't help but feel worried about her. I watched as she walked through the hallway. She's no longer rushing between the classrooms. Just checking the signs on the doors, looking for her name.

Then she stopped in on a red door. It was only diagonally across from my classroom.

She read the sign next to it and her eyes widened. Then she looked back at me then entered the red door classroom.

I breath a sigh of relief and returned back inside the class. I searched for a seat and placed myself there.

*Clock Ticking*

???: "But sir! We want to know everything about our classmate."

So the next one is... Densen.

Heads turns, looking for the person named "Densen".

I stood up and walked to the front of the class. Eyes followed me as I approached the teacher's desk. I kept my composure.

Teacher: "Well this is truly a coincidence. Seeing your surname, Densen. It truly nice to have you here in my class."

I smiled and nodded on his remarks.

Teacher: "Well please introduce yourself to the class."

I took a deep silent breath. My reharsal since earlier...

I placed my hand on my chest and gave my usual "perfect" and "charming" smile. Preparing my throat for the professional tone.

Emily: "Hello everyone, my name is Emily Densen. I will be your classmate for this school year. I am a transfer student from another school. Nice to meet you all!"

Smiling and bowing my head towards the class.

Teacher: "Well, thank you for your introduction Emily. But can you add more about yourself? Like how old are you?"

I nodded on his request. I took a deep breath and continued my introduction.

Emily: "So I am fourteen years old. I enjoy playing games and also reading books. And uhm... I can play piano, and can also dance and sing. There are more but it would be a lot."

Everyone in the class clapped and cheered on my introduction. I smiled and bowed my head again and my teacher thanked me and gestured me to return to my seat. I walked back to my seat and sat down.

Students were introduced one after another. Since it's the first day of class. The teachers didn't really gave us anything to do. Some teachers made us introduce ourselves through writing it in an index card. Time passes by quickly.

And students were staring at me most of the time. I'm somehow used to this type of attention. But still, I can't help but feel uncomfortable about it.

12:05 PM

???: "Emily? Like Emily Densen? That beautiful girl from the TV?"

I turned my head to the source of the sound. Students surrounded my table. I looked at them and smiled.

Emily: "Uhm, I guess so... Haha..."

I answered with a nervous laugh.

???: "Wow! I can't believe it! You're really Emily Densen! The one from the TV!"

???: "Is it true that you're the protagonist of 'The Below The Surface'?"

???: "Hey! Hey! Can I please have an autograph?"

???: "Hey! Is America nice?"

Students easily overwhelmed me with their questions. I smiled and tried answering them all politely.

???: "What's your favorite color?"

Emily: "My favorite color is blue!"

???: "What about your favorite food?"

Emily: "Uhm... I'm not sure, I don't eat a lot. I just eat whatever is available."

???: "Ehhhh? I don't get it... Just a food you really love to eat?"

Emily: "Nothing comes to mind."

???: "What do you do every day?"

Emily: "Well... I act in movies and TV shows. And sometimes my Mom makes me do some hobbies to practice for"

???: "You said earlier that you play games? What games do you play?"

Emily: "Well my father has a console but Mom doesn't want me to play it. I only got to play it a few times..."

The class door bursts open and hit the wall. The students turned their heads to the source of the sound. Gina entered the classroom scanning for something until her eyes landed on me. She approached me and pulled me by my arm.

Gina wasn't fazed by any of the students' protests. I was dragged outside the room until we reached the hallway. When we looked back, the students were chasing us.

???: "Hey! Wait! Where are you going?! You're taking her away! KIDNAPPER!"

Students came rushing to get me. But Gina refused to let me go. She pulled fast across the hallway. Climbing down the stairs passying by the 2nd floor from where the elementary students were.

Soon we reached the outside of the school building. The students were watching us from the windows and began chasing us. Gina pulled me again and until we reached the school cafeteria.

Gina: "Emily! We're gonna split up now. Go behind the school cafetaria. I'll lost those students."

I nodded and began running. I look back and Gina is already exhausted. But despite that she began running to the other direction. Students started chasing her.

I went behind the cafeteria and saw a broken fence. I climbed it and ran to the other side of the tall trees. Then when i entered, a boy was sitting on a tree branch, wearing a black jacket with his uniform inside. He clicks rapidly with his toy.

???: "Damn this White Dragon! I ran out of potions and pills... And buff seeds too! Seriously?"

I looked at the boy and he soon notices me. Our met and for a strange reason, as if time had stopped...
